Packet Dash

You're a data packet racing through the network. Swipe to dodge burning firewalls and malware, grab data bits, and chase your high score.

  • Every crash costs a life, and a Security+ question decides how much you get back.
  • Three modes: Password123, Two-Factor and Zero Trust.
  • Two editions: Security+ (176 questions) and CySA+ (101 questions), each with practice tests by exam domain.
