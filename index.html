About me

Control what you can control.

My motto. When it gets hard, I keep moving forward, a line from Meet the Robinsons I've held onto since I was a kid.

I didn't set out to work in IT. I chose 25H in the Army, and the mentors I met there taught me most of what I know.

They also showed me the part I enjoy most: working behind the scenes to understand how connections work and why a problem is actually happening, not just that it is. I've always wanted to know why things work the way they do. IT lets me follow that curiosity every day.

I've been through hard times and missed opportunities, some because of things I couldn't control and some because I made excuses. Looking back taught me to be grateful for what I have and to take every opportunity I get.

That's why I like teaching. I've mentored younger family members since they were in elementary school to stay focused on school and their goals, and I help anyone I can, so they get the chances I missed and don't repeat my mistakes.
