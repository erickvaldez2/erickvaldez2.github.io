About

I'm Erick Valdez, a U.S. Army network communications specialist (25H) with six years of service and hands-on experience building and maintaining tactical networks. I hold an active Secret clearance, I'm preparing for the CompTIA Security+, and I'm earning my B.S. in Cybersecurity Technology at UMGC.

My goal is to bring my military network experience into a civilian role as a network security specialist, protecting the systems organizations depend on.
